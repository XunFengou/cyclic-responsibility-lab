# Responsibility operators, coordination costs and gcd

## Problem and conventions

Fix integers N >= 3 and 1 <= k <= N. Physical robot identity is distinct from cyclic role i in Z_N. A permutation sigma assigns each active role to a physical robot. The demand measure mu has total mass 1. Positive basic atoms S_i partition the free demand domain up to measure-zero boundaries. Their masses are m_i = mu(S_i) > 0, with sum m_i = 1. Robot sigma(i) serves W_i = union of S_(i-a), a=0,...,k-1. Every demand point has exactly k responsible robots in this incidence model; this is not a claim of line-of-sight visibility.

The mass state lies in the open simplex Delta_N. The shift is (Pv)_i = v_(i-1), with indices modulo N. The responsibility map is the circulant group-algebra element R_k = I + P + ... + P^(k-1), and ell = R_k m. The group algebra is R[Z_N], equivalently R[t]/(t^N-1); polynomials in P act on R^N. This unifies the operators within a fixed population. Different populations have different-dimensional state spaces. Use the disjoint family of simplices indexed by (N,k), with explicit removal maps between fibers, rather than pretending one fixed matrix acts at every N.

## Orbit and quotient classification

Let d = gcd(N,k), q = N/d and s = k/d, so gcd(q,s)=1. The subgroup generated by k in Z_N is H = {0,d,...,(q-1)d}. Its d cosets are the residue classes modulo d; each has q elements. Indeed, adding k preserves the residue modulo d, and adding s modulo q visits every element because s is invertible modulo q. Thus Z_N/H is isomorphic to Z_d.

Subtract consecutive rows of R_k m = c*1. This gives m_i-m_(i-k)=0, hence m is constant on each coset. Conversely, a d-periodic vector has every k-window equal to (k/d) times the sum of its d coset values. Therefore balanced service loads are precisely

```text
m_i = a_(i mod d),     a_r > 0,     sum_(r=0)^(d-1) a_r = d/N.
ell_i = k/N.
```

There are d free coset values and one total-mass constraint: the balanced-service set has dimension d-1. In particular, service equality forces equal atoms exactly when d=1. On the full vector space, ker R_k consists of coset-constant vectors whose d values sum to zero, and has dimension d-1. Constants are NOT in ker R_k.

## Fourier dual and the control Laplacian

Let chi_j(i)=exp(2*pi*sqrt(-1)*j*i/N), j=0,...,N-1. These characters form an orthogonal basis over C. P chi_j = exp(-2*pi*sqrt(-1)*j/N) chi_j. The eigenvalue of R_k is

```text
r_k(0) = k;
r_k(j) = (1-exp(-2*pi*i*j*k/N))/(1-exp(-2*pi*i*j/N)), j != 0.
```

For j != 0 the denominator is nonzero. Thus r_k(j)=0 exactly when N divides jk, equivalently j is q,2q,...,(d-1)q. Complex conjugate characters pair to give the same real kernel dimension d-1; do not double this dimension.

The undirected jump Cayley graph has generator k and Laplacian Q_k=2I-P^k-P^(-k). Its eigenvalue is lambda_j=4*sin^2(pi*j*k/N). Its components are exactly the cosets above. On mean-zero states, ker Q_k equals ker R_k. On the full space, ker Q_k also contains constants and has dimension d. These distinctions explain why both Fourier zeros and disconnected jump components describe the same hidden imbalance.

The physical communication carrier G_c is a different graph. It transports packets between physical IDs, possibly over several hops. Its Laplacian L_c need not commute with P or Q_k, and a connected carrier does not remove the responsibility blind space. Conversely, a connected jump graph does not guarantee that radio packets can reach their destinations. A position-dependent radio carrier can change during motion while R_k remains unchanged. A removal changes N and the cyclic operator itself; a pure role permutation conjugates the matrix in physical-ID coordinates and preserves its spectrum.

## Coordination energies that reveal arithmetic structure

Set z = N*m-1, so z has zero mean. Define Pi_d z by averaging z within each residue class modulo d. This is the orthogonal projection onto the mean-zero blind subspace, because averaging is self-adjoint, idempotent and has exactly that range.

```text
E_mass    = ||z||^2/(2N).
E_blind   = ||Pi_d z||^2/(2N).
E_visible = E_mass - E_blind >= 0.
E_service = ||R_k z/k||^2/(2N).
E_K       = average_(h in K) ||R_h z/h||^2/(2N).
```

The displayed relative RMS mass, blind and load errors are sqrt(2*E_mass), sqrt(2*E_blind) and sqrt(2*E_service). A normalized unitary Fourier transform gives E_service = sum_(j!=0) G_j*|z_hat_j|^2/(2N), where G_j = |r_k(j)/k|^2. The fingerprint plot displays G_j, not the spectrum of a particular initial condition. Zero locations are a structural property; their excited amplitude depends on z.

For multiple orders, all summands of E_K are nonnegative. Its kernel is their common kernel; a nonzero frequency is blind exactly when N divides jh for every h. Bezout's identity shows that this is equivalent to j being a multiple of N/g, where g=gcd(N,h for h in K). Hence the common blind dimension is g-1. Adjacent orders have g=1 for every N. Adding order 1 also works. The service task stays order k; auxiliary orders alter the partition controller, not the number of service owners.

## Positive boundary motion and persistent switching

Let x_(i+N)=x_i+1 be lifted cumulative-demand boundaries and m_i=x_(i+1)-x_i. Suppose the angular demand cumulative map F is continuous and strictly increasing. The physical ray is theta_i=F^(-1)(x_i). This requires positive demand in every angular interval; current quadrature checks positive mass in every angular bin. Holes do not have to be filled, but an entirely empty angular interval breaks this coordinate hypothesis.

For a family K with positive weights gamma_h summing to 1, the implemented update is

```text
x_i^+ = x_i + beta * sum_h gamma_h
         [ell_(i+h-1)^(h) - ell_(i-1)^(h)],
m^+ = (I-beta*sum_h gamma_h Q_h)m,
0 < beta < 1/2.
```

Subtract adjacent boundary equations to verify the second identity by telescoping. Every mass update is a convex combination of m_i and its +/-h neighbors, with self weight at least 1-2*beta>0. Total mass is preserved and min_i m_i never decreases on a fixed-population run. Therefore atoms remain positive and rays cannot cross.

Every Fourier mode is multiplied by 1-beta*sum_h gamma_h lambda_j(h), whose magnitude is at most 1 and strictly less than 1 outside the common kernel. Therefore E_mass, E_blind and any fixed E_K are nonincreasing; for a service-only update E_blind is exactly invariant. With g=1, all mean-zero modes converge geometrically to zero. The boundary increments then decay geometrically, their sum is finite, and x converges including its phase. Continuous inverse F and moments yield convergent rays and centroids. This is an algebraic partition result independent of robot dynamics.

For switching, assume a finite order family, each required order used at least once in every B updates, beta bounded in a compact subinterval of (0,1/2), and active positive weights bounded away from zero. Circulants commute. For each nonconstant frequency at least one required order contracts it strictly; the finite collection gives a uniform contraction per B-update window. This proves convergence when the union has gcd 1. Use a FIXED union-order E_K for comparisons; changing the definition of the plotted objective at every switch would obscure jumps. The present switch alternates the adjacent orders in 3-motion-second blocks.

These conclusions require successful updates. A disconnected carrier or insufficient packet budget can prevent them. If infinitely many complete complementary update windows still occur, the same algebra holds in update count. A finite wall-time rate additionally needs bounded communication waiting time. The homepage uses reliable full snapshots and makes no native-protocol traffic-saving claim. The structural comparison page separately measures moment, geometry and routing costs.

## Joint N,k scale and removal

The triple (d,q,s) records quotient size, orbit length and a coprime jump inside each orbit. Q_k has the nonzero cycle spectrum 4*sin^2(pi*l/q), l=1,...,q-1, repeated d times, because multiplication by s permutes residues modulo q. This does not mean R_k has identical nonzero singular values for every k with the same d: its denominator still depends on j. With multiple generators the joint placement of Fourier eigenvalues matters, even if individual spectra agree.

For N=1000,k=21: (d,q,s)=(1,1000,21), no nonconstant blind frequency. After one announced removal N'=999: (3,333,7), blind indices 333,666. A second removal N''=998 gives (1,998,21). The gcd dimension is periodic in N modulo k, but q, spectral scale and geometry are not. At fixed finite jump lengths h, lambda_1 <= 4*pi^2*average(h^2)/N^2. Thus this family does not have a population-independent spectral gap. Nonabelian expander results cannot be imported as such a rate.

Current failure semantics remove the physical robot and its left boundary, so the two neighboring atoms merge. Other robots retain exactly their positions and IDs, and the remaining mass coordinates stay ordered. The cyclic fiber changes from Delta_N to Delta_(N-1); the mass map is positive and sum-preserving, not an invertible coordinate change. Keep k <= N-1. A parked failed robot would require a new obstacle and a different safety model; it is not simulated here.

If masses were exactly 1/N before a single removal, afterward one atom is 2/N and the others 1/N. For d'=gcd(N-1,k), projecting onto the d' cosets gives a limiting single-order relative mass error sqrt(d'-1)/N. To derive it, the coset containing the doubled atom has a projected relative offset (d'-1)/N; the other d'-1 cosets have offset -1/N. Averaging their squares yields (d'-1)/N^2. Thus N50->49,k7 has residue sqrt(6)/50; N1000->999,k21 has sqrt(2)/1000. This formula assumes exact equal pre-fault masses and one merged atom. Several failures or an already unequal state require the actual projected vector; gcd alone gives no residual amplitude. Complementary orders 21,22 remove the new common blind space for N999.

## Distance coverage cost and physical limitations

Let b_i and h_i be the first and squared-radius moments of W_i. The geometric cost and unconstrained centroid bound are

```text
H(P,x) = (1/(2k)) sum_i integral_(W_i) ||y-p_sigma(i)||^2 dmu(y).
c_i = b_i/ell_i.
H_star(x) = (1/(2k)) sum_i [h_i-||b_i||^2/ell_i].
H-H_star = (1/(2k)) sum_i ell_i ||p_sigma(i)-c_i||^2.
```

At fixed P, nearest-k Voronoi minimizes H pointwise. A lower H for Voronoi is expected; cyclic responsibility offers an explicit incidence template and repair law. It does not prove superior distance coverage. The website therefore keeps these measurements separate.

H cannot identify gcd. On a uniform unit disk take k=N and distinct robots evenly spaced on a ring of radius r>0. Every robot serves the whole disk, whose centroid is zero and second moment is 1/2. Direct substitution gives H=1/4+r^2/2 for every N and every partition, while d=N and the blind dimension is N-1. For r=0.7, H=0.495 for both N=18 and N=24. This is an exact counterexample, not a fitted trend.

For fixed rays, unconstrained single-integrator descent p_dot=a(c-p) gives H_dot=-(a/k)*sum_i ell_i*||p_i-c_i||^2. In a convex disk it preserves containment. With moving boundaries, H has an additional boundary term of either sign. With holes and collision constraints, centroids may be infeasible, and the safety projection can stop a robot at a constrained equilibrium. The current safe baseline checks swept paths and permits a zero command. It proves numerical feasibility of accepted steps under the circular-obstacle model; it does NOT prove convergence to all Euclidean centroids, global optimality, or persistent visibility. Even vanishing control and equal masses do not remove this limitation.

- Pure blind harmonic: N24,k6, relative mass RMS 0.25. The service error is zero and blind energy is 0.03125; service-only control cannot change it. Adding order 7 removes it.
- Coprime N25,k6: no exact blind root, although a small visible gain can make a mode slow.
- Failure N50->49,k7: compare equal initial masses, identical random robot positions, then service-only, simultaneous complement and persistent switching. Use exact pre/post event snapshots.
- Large N1000,k21: use the arithmetic lab, not 1000 robots in the expensive physical canvas. Compare N999,998 and service/complement spectra; physical realization is a separate scale study.
