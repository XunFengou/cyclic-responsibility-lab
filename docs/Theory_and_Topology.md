# Distributed cyclic responsibility: model, theorem, and topology

This is a precise baseline for a paper and a working reference implementation. It separates statements proved for the model, numerical observations, and extensions that need a new theorem. All indices below are modulo the current number N unless a lifted boundary index is explicitly used.

## 1. Three state spaces, rather than one graph

**Physical space.** The workspace is a disk or a star-shaped annular band

\[
\Omega=\{(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta):r_-(\theta)\le r\le r_+(\theta)\}.
\]

The demand density rho is known, static, bounded, and positive. Its normalized total mass is 1. Define angular demand and its lifted cumulative map by

\[
w(\theta)=\int_{r_-(\theta)}^{r_+(\theta)}\rho(r,\theta)r\,dr,
\quad F(\theta)=\int_0^\theta w(a)\,da,
\quad F(\theta+2\pi)=F(\theta)+1.
\]

Assume 0 < w_min <= w(theta) <= w_max. The annular hole is allowed; arbitrary disconnected angular slices are outside this baseline.

**Partition state.** There are N independent virtual rays, with lifted angles theta_0 < ... < theta_(N-1) < theta_0+2pi. Their cumulative coordinates are x_i=F(theta_i), with x_(i+N)=x_i+1. Basic atom S_i lies between theta_i and theta_(i+1). Define its mass m_i=x_(i+1)-x_i, first moment b_i=integral over S_i of z rho(z) dz, and second moment h_i=integral over S_i of ||z||^2 rho(z) dz. Thus m belongs to the open simplex Delta_N={m_i>0, sum m_i=1}. The lifted x state also fixes a phase, which m alone does not determine.

**Role and robot spaces.** The cyclic role group is C_N=Z/NZ. The active robot IDs form a separate set V. A bijection sigma:C_N -> V assigns roles to physical robots. A robot has position p_v in R^2 and, in the annulus implementation, a lifted polar navigation state. Virtual rays are not constrained to pass through the robots. That additional constraint would change the feasibility problem.

For changing N, use a hybrid family of these state spaces, with explicit reset maps between different dimensions. There is no canonical identification of C_1000 and C_999. The parameter stratum (d,q,s), where d=gcd(N,k), q=N/d, s=k/d and gcd(q,s)=1, records quotient size, orbit length, and the marked jump. It does not by itself identify physical failure positions or a communication graph.

## 2. Responsibility operator and quotient classification

For 1 <= k <= N, define retrospective responsibility

\[
W_i^{(k)}=\bigcup_{a=0}^{k-1}S_{i-a},\quad
\ell_i^{(k)}=\sum_{a=0}^{k-1}m_{i-a},\quad
c_i^{(k)}={\sum_{a=0}^{k-1}b_{i-a}\over\ell_i^{(k)}}.
\]

P is the cyclic shift (Pm)_i=m_(i-1). The responsibility operator is the circulant

\[
R_{N,k}=I+P+\cdots+P^{k-1},\qquad \ell^{(k)}=R_{N,k}m.
\]

It is multiplication by 1+z+...+z^(k-1) in the group algebra R[C_N]=R[z]/(z^N-1). The responsibility hypergraph has atoms as vertices and windows W_i as hyperedges. Every atom belongs to exactly k windows.

**Theorem 1 (classification).** Let H=<k> be the subgroup generated by k in C_N. Then H=d C_N, |H|=q, C_N/H is isomorphic to C_d, and:

1. The jump i -> i+k has exactly d orbits, the residue classes i mod d, each of length q.
2. All service loads equal k/N if and only if m_i=a_(i mod d), where a_r>0 and q sum_(r=0)^(d-1) a_r=1.
3. dim ker R_(N,k)=d-1 over R or C. The fixed-total equal-load family has dimension d-1.

**Proof.** Write N=dq and k=ds. A jump preserves i mod d, and within one residue class becomes addition by s modulo q. Since gcd(q,s)=1 it visits all q indices. Further,

\[
(I-P)R_{N,k}=I-P^k.
\]

Equal loads therefore imply m_i=m_(i-k), hence constancy on the d orbits. Conversely, k consecutive indices contain each residue modulo d exactly s times. Their load is s sum a_r=k/N. For a kernel vector the same orbit constancy holds and its window sum is s sum a_r=0, one independent constraint on d numbers. This proves the dimension. Positivity merely restricts the equilibrium family to the interior of its simplex. QED.

Let E_d average a vector within each residue class modulo d, and J=(1/N)11^T. Then E_d-J projects onto the nonconstant blind space. In particular, a balanced responsibility signal need not determine a unique partition when d>1.

## 3. Fourier dual and the precise role of s

For frequency j=0,...,N-1, let f_j(i)=exp(2pi sqrt(-1) j i/N). Then P f_j=exp(-2pi sqrt(-1)j/N)f_j. The responsibility multiplier is

\[
r_k(j)=\sum_{a=0}^{k-1}e^{-2\pi\mathrm{i}ja/N}.
\]

r_k(0)=k. For j != 0 it vanishes exactly when jk is divisible by N, equivalently j is a nonzero multiple of q. Thus the blind frequencies are q,2q,...,(d-1)q. They are precisely the nontrivial characters of C_N/H. This is the Fourier version of the quotient theorem, not an independent numerical coincidence.

The boundary-control Laplacian is

\[
Q_k=(I-P^k)^*(I-P^k)=2I-P^k-P^{-k},\qquad
\lambda_k(j)=4\sin^2(\pi jk/N).
\]

It is the weighted Cayley multigraph Laplacian for steps +/-k. For k=N it is zero; for k=N/2 the coincident directions have multiplicity two. Inside each orbit its eigenvalues are those of a q-cycle: multiplication by s permutes the labelled frequencies modulo q. Hence equal d and q give equal unordered spectra for Q, while the slow physical Fourier frequencies, R multipliers, and routing lengths can differ. The smallest positive orbit eigenvalue for q>=2 is 4 sin^2(pi/q); it is not the mean-zero gap when d>1, which is zero.

At N=1000,k=21: (d,q,s)=(1,1000,21), no blind frequency, and gap(Q_21)=4 sin^2(pi/1000), approximately 3.94783e-5. The slow labels include j=381 and 619, since 21*381=1 mod 1000. At N=999,k=21: (3,333,7), with blind labels 333 and 666. At N=1008,k=21: (21,48,1), with 20 blind labels. Coprimality gives uniqueness; it does not give a scale-independent speed.

## 4. A local boundary law with a positive mass realization

Let K be a set of control orders, gamma_k>=0, sum gamma_k=1. These may include auxiliary orders while the service task remains at one fixed order k_service. With gain kappa>0, use

\[
\dot x_i=\kappa\sum_{k\in K}\gamma_k
 (\ell_{i+k-1}^{(k)}-\ell_{i-1}^{(k)}),
\qquad \dot\theta_i=\dot x_i/w(\theta_i).
\]

The implemented sample update is the same formula with dot x replaced by (x_i^+-x_i)/dt. Let beta=kappa dt. It uses the current complete snapshot, with 0<beta<1/2 (the experiments use beta=0.2). Derivation gives

\[
m_i^+=(1-2\beta)m_i+
\beta\sum_{k\in K}\gamma_k(m_{i+k}+m_{i-k})
=[(I-\beta\sum_k\gamma_kQ_k)m]_i.
\]

The controller never needs to construct this global vector. The equation is an analysis identity, checked independently against routed local messages.

**Theorem 2 (feasibility and complementarity).** Under the stated snapshot and gain assumptions, every atom remains nonempty; total mass and the lifted phase sum_i x_i are preserved. For fixed positive weights and N, the mass converges to E_g m(0), where g=gcd(N,{k:gamma_k>0}). It converges to 1/N for every initial mass vector if and only if g=1. Every service window then has load k_service/N.

**Proof.** The update is a convex combination, so min_i m_i^+ >= min_i m_i>0. Its row and column sums are one. Summing boundary increments telescopes, preserving phase. Ordered x and strict monotonicity of F give ordered theta; indeed each angular width is at least min m(0)/w_max. The Fourier multipliers are 1-beta a_j, where a_j=sum gamma_k lambda_k(j). For 0<beta<1/2 their modulus is strictly less than one whenever a_j>0, and is one exactly when jk=0 mod N for every active k. Those common zeros are j multiples of N/g. The limit projector is E_g. It equals J exactly when g=1. QED.

The positivity statement also holds at beta=1/2, but unconditional convergence does not: a bipartite walk may oscillate. This is why the theorem uses a strict inequality.

**Universal arithmetic repair.** If N>=k+1, the pair {k,k+1} is complementary for every N because gcd(k,k+1)=1. At N=k>=2, use {k,k-1}. This is a simple membership-robust arithmetic policy; its routing and moment costs still have to fit the communication budget. It is a sufficient policy, not necessarily the best spectral or energy choice.

The continuous-time mass equation is dot m=-kappa sum gamma_k Q_k m, with the same limiting projector. Define E(m)=1/2 sum gamma_k ||R_k m-(k/N)1||^2. Because x -> m has a cycle-difference derivative, the x law is negative Euclidean gradient flow of E in cumulative coordinates. Equivalently L_1 R_k^*R_k=Q_k, where L_1=2I-P-P^(-1). The incidence/geometry factor is essential; R_k^*R_k alone is not the boundary mass generator.

## 5. Persistent switching and communication budgets

For time-dependent normalized weights gamma_k(t), all these cyclic operators share a Fourier basis. In discrete epochs t=0,1,... each mode has the exact solution

\[
\widehat m_j(T)=\widehat m_j(0)\prod_{t=0}^{T-1}
 [1-\beta\sum_k\gamma_k(t)\lambda_k(j)].
\]

For fixed 0<beta<1/2, a sufficient condition for full balancing is sum_t sum_k gamma_k(t) lambda_k(j)=infinity for every j != 0. A finite repeatedly visited family with a uniformly positive weight per visit has this property precisely when its joint gcd with N is one. Bounded revisit intervals give a uniform contraction per block of epochs. Infinite but arbitrarily sparse visits give no uniform time rate. A one-time use of a complementary order is generally insufficient. The exact product, rather than a union-of-edges picture alone, specifies the exceptional finite annihilation cases.

After 50 -> 49 at service order 7, the baseline alternates control order 7 and 8 every four motion seconds. It preserves the same service task and removes the joint blind space. At an instant with order 7, the instantaneous gcd is still 7; this does not contradict convergence of the persistent family.

For candidate orders k and estimated costs c_k, normalized stationary weight design can maximize the certified per-motion-time gap through the linear program

\[
\max_{\gamma,z}z:\quad
 z\le\sum_k\gamma_k\lambda_k(j)\ (j=1,...,N-1),\quad
 \gamma\ge0,\quad\sum\gamma=1,\quad\sum c_k\gamma_k\le B.
\]

This coercivity design is a correct linear budget relaxation when c_k is the cost of selecting a single-order epoch and gamma describes time sharing. For the implemented beta=0.2, nonnegative Fourier multipliers give a block contraction bound from the accumulated eigenvalues; the exact product can give a stronger rate. Simultaneous batched protocols can share moments but send wider packets; their cost is a set function, not this linear expression. The engine measures their actual model hop costs. For elapsed-time optimization, use the contraction product divided by motion time plus measured/assumed communication duration, and include discovery and reassignment costs. Any claimed scale advantage must state whether degree, payload, rounds, range, or total bytes are budgeted.

## 6. The carrier graph is a separate operator

Let Pi_sigma map a role-indexed vector into robot-ID order. With columns still indexed by geometric atoms, the responsibility incidence is A_sigma=Pi_sigma R_k. Its rows, and not its columns, change when robots exchange roles. The robot-to-robot jump overlay is Pi_sigma Q_k Pi_sigma^T. Only a simultaneous reindexing of both domain and codomain gives a conjugate Pi R Pi^T. Confusing these operations hides the difference between a new assignment and a new service task.

Let G_c(p,t)=(V,E_c(p,t)) be the actual undirected carrier graph, B_c an oriented vertex-edge incidence matrix, and L_c=B_c B_c^T its Laplacian. The reference protocol computes atom moments locally, forwards them along role successors, then forwards the required load differences. Every logical packet is routed over carrier edges, using locally received link-state maps. The testbed rejects stale epochs and nonexistent hops, and includes topology floods in the cost.

If every snapshot is delivered before an update, changing G_c changes routing cost and elapsed time; the logical mass operator remains Q_K. It does not become L_c simply because the carrier changed.

In a different algorithm that measures only carrier-neighbor load differences, the observation matrix is

\[
C=B_c^T\Pi_\sigma R_k,\qquad C^*C=R_k^*\Pi_\sigma^T L_c\Pi_\sigma R_k.
\]

If the carrier is connected, ker C={m:R_k m is constant}; restricted to zero-total perturbations this is ker R_k, so adding carrier edges alone cannot repair a service blind mode. If the carrier has components V_a, its kernel instead allows one independent load level per component, subject to the image of R_k. The positive-semidefinite Gram matrix gives observability, but using it as a mass generator need not preserve positive masses: its off-diagonal signs are not automatically those of a graph Laplacian. A new boundary law and invariant-set proof would be required.

Use the hybrid parameter state (N,k,sigma,G_c), with permutation actions and explicit graph incidence matrices at each fixed N. A circulant group-algebra subfamily is available only when cyclic symmetry is present. General distance graphs are not circulants. Quotienting a labelled state by cyclic rotation forgets an arbitrary starting ray; quotienting by all permutations generally forgets physical adjacency and routing costs that matter.

## 7. Two failure responsibility models

**Rebuilt atoms (implemented).** At an announced membership event remove the failed robot and its owned ray. Adjacent masses merge without moving surviving robots. There is a positive column-sum-one merge matrix M_F, with exactly one 1 in each column, such that m_new=M_F m_old. All retained atoms have positive mass, and new responsibility is R_(N-|F|,k) on the surviving cyclic ray order. Failure positions determine M_F. They affect which blind components are excited even when the new gcd is identical. This merge is generally not a group homomorphism, and M_F R_(N,k)=R_(N-|F|,k) M_F is generally false.

For equal pre-failure masses 1/50 and a single removed ray, one new mass is 2/50 and the other 48 masses are 1/50. At N_new=49,k=7, the limiting orbit means are one class at 8/350 and six at 7/350. Their relative RMS mass error is sqrt(6)/50=0.0489897949, while every service load is exactly 7/49. Orders 7 and 8 together have gcd 1 and drive all masses to 1/49.

For 1000 -> 999 with k=21, one ray deletion changes gcd 1 -> 3 and creates two possible blind directions. Which of the three residue classes inherits the merged excess depends on failure position. A second deletion gives N=998 with gcd 1 and makes uniqueness return. Adding or removing a healthy robot to change the gcd consumes a resource; it is not a free algebraic repair.

More generally, a single deletion from N0 equal pre-failure masses gives a single-order limiting relative mass error sqrt(d-1)/N0, where d=gcd(N0-1,k). For N0=1000,k=21 this is sqrt(2)/1000. Rotating the failed ray changes the Fourier phase, while leaving this magnitude unchanged in mass coordinates. That equality does not extend to arbitrary nonuniform pre-failure masses.

**Fixed atoms (not the implemented fault model).** If N original atoms remain, robot loss deletes rows of R or invokes a new rectangular reassignment matrix W_F. The operator is A_F=W_F R_(N,k), not R_(N-|F|,k). There is no general gcd(N-|F|,k) theorem for its kernel. Rank, nonnegative allocation feasibility, and communication reachability must be checked for that actual incidence. The two models answer different questions.

**Corollary (finite reconstruction).** With finitely many announced failures and valid role transfers, each merge preserves positive masses and total coverage. After the last event, any fixed or persistent complementary control satisfying Theorem 2 or Section 5 balances the remaining basic atoms. With an infinite fault stream, or no stable membership, position convergence is not implied by this corollary.

## 8. Robot positions, arrangement, and the meaning of higher order

In the convex disk use the requested single-integrator law dot p_(sigma(i))=eta(c_i^(k_service)-p_(sigma(i))), eta>0. With a target held for dt, the implementation uses its exact solution p^+=c+exp(-eta dt)(p-c). Convexity keeps positions inside the disk. After the last membership or assignment event, convergence of m together with preservation of sum x uniquely determines a convergent lifted boundary vector. Positive service masses then make the moment ratio continuous, so c_i converges. The stable filter gives p_(sigma(i)) -> c_i^*. Proof: the tracking error is a decaying homogeneous term plus the convolution of the stable exponential kernel with a vanishing target error. The same conclusion holds for held targets converging along infinitely many motion epochs.

The physical service cost is J=1/2 sum_i integral over W_i of ||z-p_(sigma(i))||^2 rho(z) dz. With fixed W_i, a centroid minimizes that robot's term. The reported cost is J/k to account for k-fold responsibility. The boundary mass-balancing energy in Section 4 is a different objective; these two controllers do not automatically make a common physical cost decrease, and the limiting configuration is not claimed to be a globally optimal coverage configuration.

In a uniform unit disk with equal angular atoms and omega=2pi k/N,

\[
|c_i|={2\over3}{\sin(\omega/2)\over\omega/2},\qquad
\arg c_i=\theta_i+(1-k/2){2\pi\over N},\quad k<N.
\]

This proves a natural cyclic arrangement at equilibrium for this symmetric geometry. At k=N all targets are the origin. Higher order provides overlapping responsibility and redundant service assignment; it does not guarantee distinct robot positions, better sensing quality, or physical k-fold sensor coverage. A sensing-footprint model would add those requirements. This definition is a cyclic analogue of higher-order assignment, not the nearest-k Voronoi diagram.

For an annulus the Euclidean centroid can lie in the hole and a straight segment can cross the hole. The viewer therefore uses a different controller: radially clip the raw target to r_-+delta <= r <= r_+-delta, express positions as (alpha,u) with lifted angle alpha and normalized radial fraction 0<=u<=1, and use dot alpha=eta(alpha_target-alpha), dot u=eta(u_target-u). Mapping back yields a physical single-integrator input equal to the chart Jacobian times this velocity. The mapped path stays in the annulus by construction. This is not unmodified Euclidean centroid descent and need not decrease the Euclidean coverage cost. Conditional convergence follows when the feasible target has a convergent continuous angle lift; in particular, it holds locally around a nonzero limiting raw centroid with exponential boundary convergence. A zero raw centroid or an ill-defined desired angle requires a separate target rule. The reported N50/k7 runs stay in the regular regime.

Nonempty virtual sectors and collision-free point ordering are different properties. Neither robot controller implements pairwise collision avoidance. Sampled separation is reported as a diagnostic, not a safety certificate. A barrier/QP controller, coupled robot-angle boundaries, parked failures, arbitrary obstacles, limited sensing, delays, and packet loss all require additional feasibility and stability work. The application blocks an incomplete disconnected epoch rather than extending its theorem silently.

## 9. Explicit distributed protocol and accounting

An epoch starts with fixed positions, membership, role assignment, and current virtual boundaries. An owner obtains its successor boundary by a routed packet and integrates its own atom. Four scalar moments (m,b_x,b_y,h) are circulated along role successors for max(k_service,K)-1 stages, producing exact overlapping window sums without a centralized mass input. Control loads are passed once to successors and up to max(K)-1 times to predecessors. This yields exactly ell_(i-1) and ell_(i+k-1) at boundary owner i. All fields are tagged by the epoch; a barrier completes the snapshot before control.

The routing service floods each robot's neighbor list to all others. Each source constructs routes from its received map. Static ideal ring/path maps are reused until a topology or membership change; the moving radio graph deliberately repeats discovery each epoch. The latter is a simple correct baseline, not an efficient routing claim. Angle-ranked reassignment uses an additional all-gather of local angles and a boundary-state transfer. Its chosen cyclic phase is deterministic, not optimized for distance or energy.

For identity role ownership on the ideal ring, r simultaneous control orders, a=max(k_service,K), b=max(K), the per-epoch control cost excluding discovery is N(a+b) single-hop packets and N[1+4(a-1)+r b] double-precision payload scalars. An assumed 24-byte header is charged per hop. General assignments and paths multiply costs by actual route lengths and add edge-capacity queueing. There is at most one packet per directed carrier edge per round. Payload sizes vary; this is not a fixed-bit-capacity or packet-fragmentation model. The elapsed clock adds communication rounds times the assumed round duration to the motion clock. Connectivity within an epoch is assumed, with positions frozen during communication. General jointly connected asynchronous operation is not implemented.

## 11. Appropriate theoretical sources

- [Cortes et al., Coverage Control for Mobile Sensing Networks, author copy](https://motion.me.ucsb.edu/pdf/2002j-cmkb.pdf): centroid coverage framework. Its Voronoi partition law is not a proof of this cyclic higher-order boundary theorem.
- [Olfati-Saber and Murray, agreement with switching topology, Caltech research page](https://murray.cds.caltech.edu/Agreement_Problems_in_Networks_with_Directed_Graphs_and_Switching_Toplogy): graph consensus background. Routing a fixed logical operator must be distinguished from changing the consensus operator.
- [Moreau, Stability of multiagent systems with time-dependent communication links](https://biblio.ugent.be/publication/309693): switching connectivity background; the simple common Fourier basis makes the present cyclic switching calculation more explicit.
- [Higher-order Voronoi coverage research preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.1941): comparison with nearest-neighbor higher-order spatial assignments.
- [Cortes et al., limited-range interactions, author copy](https://motion.me.ucsb.edu/pdf/2004e-cmb.pdf): why finite-range sensing and communication introduce additional constraints.

The quotient, Fourier, positive realization, and protocol identities above are derived explicitly here. The literature supplies context, not a claim that this research baseline is already a complete obstacle-aware, collision-free, asynchronous distributed controller.
