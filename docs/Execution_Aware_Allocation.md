# Execution-aware distributed K-th allocation

## Why balanced loads produced only 12.44 percent

The preserved TIAGo baseline equalizes assigned mass, not visible, executable work. A robot can own a target behind its camera, behind an obstacle, beyond range, above its head-tilt range, or while its execution channel is occupied. Two assigned robots can also provide nearly collinear views. No mass-balancing theorem excludes these situations.

The same-trajectory replay reproduces the original 30 s, N=6,k=2 planar two-view mean 0.12441498360054606. Under the same positions, headings, range, horizontal FOV and obstacle geometry, routed feasibility repair attains mean 0.25127901005375164; nearest-k attains 0.1309007657973406. All capped policies use the same per-robot demand budget 1.35*k/N. In this recording repair reaches the uncapped current-pose ceiling, so changing owners alone cannot remove the remaining gap. This is an empirical statement for this trajectory, not a performance bound for the greedy allocator.

The closed-loop experiment changes the mission to ten finite, weighted 3D inspection jobs. Its cumulative completion fraction is a DIFFERENT measurement. A result of 100% cumulative finite-job completion must not be described as raising the original continuous-domain instantaneous coverage from 12.44% to 100%.

## State spaces and units

Let I=Z/NZ be cyclic roles; let physical IDs persist across failure and reindexing. A bijection pi:I->active IDs assigns roles to robots. N is the number of active complete mobile manipulators. k is the required number of distinct physical robot observers, 1<=k<=N. Two arms of one TIAGo are internal resources of ONE agent; they never satisfy k=2 independent robots.

The known static planar domain is a radius-10 m disk minus three circular holes. Its positive demand density is normalized to mass one. Virtual radial boundaries are represented in cumulative angular mass coordinates x_0<...<x_(N-1)<x_0+1; m_i=x_(i+1)-x_i with x_N=x_0+1. Thus m lies in the open probability simplex. Rays are independent of robot polar angles. A base safety radius of 0.55 m is an explicit circular footprint approximation; it does not describe the entire arm workspace.

Each robot has q_i=(base x,y,yaw,torso,head pan,head tilt,left arm 7,right arm 7) and velocity v_i. Positions in exported scene maps are normalized by 10 m; q, camera poses and physical metrics use SI units. Inspection targets j=1,...,M have horizontal point p_j, height z_j, positive normalized weight w_j, soft due-time priority D_j, required sampled dwell T_j=0.6 s, progress h_j, and a completion flag. Due times prioritize jobs; they are not hard-deadline guarantees.

The complete state also includes robot capabilities c_i, declared resource reservations, a communication carrier G(t), local received-message tables, assignment version, lease expiry and outstanding jobs. This is a hybrid state over disjoint strata indexed by N and active physical-ID sets; it is not one fixed-dimensional vector space when robots are removed.

## Unified cyclic operator and its exact blind space

Define the permutation (Pu)_i=u_(i-1 mod N). In the real group algebra of Z/NZ, R_k=I+P+...+P^(k-1). Row i sums the k consecutive basic atom masses owned by robot pi(i): ell=R_k m. With this convention atom j has owners j,...,j+k-1 modulo N. Total assigned mass is k, so the equal-load target is k/N.

**Theorem 1.** Let d=gcd(N,k), N=dq, k=ds and gcd(q,s)=1. There are d orbits of adding k modulo N, each of length q. The orbit set is the quotient Z/NZ / <k>, isomorphic to Z/dZ. R_k m is constant exactly when m_i=a_(i mod d). Its kernel consists of these periodic vectors with sum_(r=0)^(d-1) a_r=0. Hence dim ker R_k=d-1.

**Proof.** The identity (I-P)R_k=I-P^k implies that constant R_k m gives m_i=m_(i-k). Adding k preserves residues modulo d. After division by d, adding s modulo q visits every element because s is invertible modulo q, proving the orbit and quotient claims. Each length-k window has exactly s entries from each residue class; consequently R_k m=s*(sum a_r)*1 for any orbit-constant m. Setting this to zero imposes one independent linear equation on d free numbers. Conversely the shift identity shows every kernel vector is orbit-constant. For unit total mass q*sum a_r=1, so the common load is s/q=k/N.

Let f_l(i)=exp(2*pi*sqrt(-1)*l*i/N), l=0,...,N-1. P f_l=exp(-2*pi*sqrt(-1)*l/N) f_l. These vectors diagonalize every circulant operator. The R_k eigenvalue is r_k(l)=sum_(h=0)^(k-1) exp(-2*pi*sqrt(-1)*lh/N). At l=0 it is k. For l!=0 the geometric sum vanishes iff N divides kl, so the blind Fourier indices are q,2q,...,(d-1)q. Their real dimension is also d-1, including conjugate pairing. This proves that quotient/orbit and Fourier descriptions are the same blind space in primal and dual coordinates.

For the boundary graph L_k=2I-P^k-P^(-k), eigenvalues are 4*sin^2(pi*k*l/N). Its kernel is the d-dimensional orbit-constant space, including the constant vector; restricting to zero-sum mass deviations removes that one direction. A communication Laplacian L_G acts on message/consensus states and generally has different edges. It commutes with P and shares this Fourier basis only when its carrier weights are circulant. A connected physical carrier can route an R_k interaction without making the blind space disappear.

**Theorem 2.** For positive weights alpha_h and service orders S, the joint zero-sum blind space of all R_h has dimension gcd(N,S)-1. The boundary generator sum_h alpha_h L_h has the same zero-sum kernel. In particular consecutive orders k,k+1 remove all arithmetic blindness.

**Proof.** The frequency l is blind to every order exactly when N divides h*l for every h in S. Bezout's identity gives the equivalent condition N divides gcd(N,S)*l. There are gcd(N,S)-1 nonzero such frequencies. Alternatively, a sum of positive semidefinite graph Laplacians has kernel equal to the intersection of their kernels. Its connected components are cosets of the subgroup generated by S. This establishes complementarity; it does not imply that every physically constrained inspection job is feasible.

The joint-scale description (d,q,s) separates multiplicity d from the reduced primitive pair (q,s). With only order k, each component is a q-cycle up to relabeling, with component spectral gap 4*sin^2(pi/q). Increasing q worsens that gap. An expander theorem for another family of non-Abelian groups does not establish a size-independent rate for these fixed-generator cycles.

For positive alpha_h summing to one, the implemented reference boundary input is xdot_i=sum_h alpha_h[(R_h m)_(i+h-1)-(R_h m)_(i-1)]. Taking consecutive boundary differences gives mdot=-sum_h alpha_h L_h m. Its explicit step of size delta is m_i^+=(1-2*delta)m_i+delta*sum_h alpha_h(m_(i-h)+m_(i+h)). For0<delta<1/2 this preserves strict positivity and total mass, so basic sectors retain positive mass. The implemented delta=0.1 satisfies this condition. On the shared static map, positive angular demand gives nonempty geometric sectors. In the connected complementary case every nonconstant Fourier multiplier lies strictly inside the unit circle, proving reference-mass convergence for fixed N. This proof is about virtual boundaries; it does not certify obstacle-safe physical convergence, camera feasibility or task completion. Removal changes N and starts a new reference stratum.

For N=1000,k=21: d=1,q=1000,s=21 and no arithmetic blind direction. One removal gives N=999,d=3,q=333,s=7 and two blind directions at Fourier indices 333 and 666. N=998 has d=1; N=987 has d=21 and twenty blind directions. Adding order22 removes the common blind space in all these cases because gcd(21,22)=1. This says nothing by itself about cameras behind walls. The physical 20-DoF browser experiment is restricted to N<=8; the algebra explorer supports large N.

## Effective task hypergraph and feasibility

Let A_ij(t) be the binary committed robot-to-job incidence. A pending column may have zero owners; a committed unfinished job has exactly k distinct owners. This N by M matrix is not the square R_k operator. Its feasible hyperedges are k-robot coalitions with compatible prospective viewing poses. A single robot's admissible bids require availability, a positive capacity slot and actuation reserve, a navigable base path, torso/head limits, and a predicted observation height within a conservative vertical-FOV margin.

The actual observation predicate e_ij(t) is evaluated from the integrated MuJoCo head transform with an explicit virtual camera mount, range7 m, horizontal FOV120 degrees and vertical FOV60 degrees. The camera mount is an experiment assumption, not a calibrated hardware camera. Obstacles are vertical cylinders of height1.6 m: exact segment-cylinder intersection determines actual 3D LOS. Prospective bid generation conservatively requires planar LOS, so it can reject a viewpoint that would see over an obstacle. At least one observer pair must satisfy abs(sin(bearing difference))>=sin(25 degrees); k=3 means three visible assigned robots and at least one noncollinear pair, not three pairwise-separated views.

Head/torso ranges and full articulated physical dynamics constrain inspection. We do not use arm IK to certify camera visibility. The arms are held in posture during inspection; separate dual-arm transport explicitly checks and integrates hand/object constraints. Payload mass changes dynamics, while speed, chassis force, software joint limits and resource capacities model heterogeneous execution ability. The actuation-reserve and ETA estimates are heuristics, not a reachability or deadline proof.

Default concurrent processing capacity is two jobs; even IDs have one. Even IDs carry a modeled 2 kg tool payload, have reduced chassis force and speed, ID3 has a restricted head tilt and ID5 a reduced torso range. ID2 is unavailable during [12,27) s. This is a declared resource booking corresponding in duration to the separate transport experiment. It is NOT a tray co-simulation inside the multi-robot map.

The preferred cyclic owner field is C(p). Repair first searches owners near that field, then expands to the full active fleet if necessary. Minimizing travel/ETA and residual load competes with a cyclic-membership preference and minimum owner-hold time. The implemented finite-candidate greedy search is not a complete solver for continuous constrained coverage. It can miss a feasible coalition or leave a job pending. No universal completion, optimality or scale-independent convergence theorem is claimed.

For a sampled continuous map, aggregate repaired responsibilities by basic atom to obtain an N by N matrix B. The normalized shift commutator norm ||BP-PB||_F/max(1,||B||_F), measured rank and changed membership mass show how geometry breaks cyclic symmetry. Masking or reconstruction generally makes this norm nonzero. The gcd theorem continues to classify the reference R_k, while rank(B), feasible hyperedges and carrier cuts classify execution. They must not be conflated.

## Routed dynamic reconstruction

Every one-second allocation epoch uses the following reliable synchronous protocol. Robots exchange timestamped snapshots over the selected carrier. The coordinator of job j is the active robot at role (j-1) mod N. A serialized remaining-capacity token visits coordinators in soft-priority order. Each coordinator requests and receives private feasibility bids, selects from its received mailbox, deducts capacity and records prospective viewing poses. Candidate sets first follow cyclic proximity for cyclic repair; the strong geometric baseline searches the full fleet without that preference.

The demand map, job catalogue, initial cyclic reference and synchronous clock are shared experiment inputs. The autonomous mass reference is deterministically replicated from that known map; this page does not charge an additional private-map distributed boundary-estimation protocol. Its traffic comparison measures the implemented state/bid/allocation/evidence protocol, not a complete ROS middleware stack or unknown-map learning system. Robot occlusion and self-occlusion are not modeled by the obstacle-cylinder LOS test.

The final decision encodes all selected poses, owner IDs, versions, costs and lease fields into routed prepare packets. Each resource owner checks its assigned-slot count, returns ACK and installs the prepared decision only after a complete commit broadcast. An unaffordable batch is deferred before any of its packets are sent; actual routed bytes and rounds must equal the precomputed routing charge. Earlier discovery/bidding overhead is still charged. Complete reliable batches are a model assumption, not a solution to asynchronous atomic-commit failure.

A failed allocation attempt retains the previous valid lease until its three-second expiry. After expiry the robot reference becomes its current position and damping brakes its real inertial state. Its velocity and position are never frozen or reset. A reservation immediately blocks observations and processing even if an old plan still names the robot. Removed robots disappear from the modeled physical domain; this is announced removal, not a parked disabled obstacle. Surviving q/v are copied exactly into the rebuilt plant, roles are reindexed, leases invalidated and bids reconstructed.

Each 0.1 s sample routes actual observation evidence to the job coordinator, including timestamp and assignment lease version. Processing is truncated to the robot's current capacity. A missing, stale, invisible, blocked or noncollinear sample resets progress. Six consecutive successful sampled reports provide the 0.6 s dwell test. Completion becomes globally known only after its announcement reaches every robot. This is a sampled-data certificate; it does not prove continuous visibility between samples.

**Proposition 3, conditional protocol safety.** In a successful reliable epoch, every installed job has k distinct owners, every resource owner has at most its advertised slot count, and no partially prepared plan is installed. Each completed job has received the required sampled evidence and a complete notification. A failed communication epoch cannot fabricate service evidence.

**Proof.** The single token starts with nonnegative advertised capacities. Each selected distinct owner has positive remaining capacity and is decremented once, so induction over jobs establishes the capacity invariant. Prepare carries the entire decision; all resource ACKs are required before commit, and commit is charged and delivered as a complete reliable batch. Hence all installed copies have the same version. The observation predicate, freshness/version checks and capacity truncation precede dwell increments; every missed sample resets dwell. A completed flag requires dwell plus all completion recipients. Failure branches add no evidence and cannot satisfy this predicate. Sudden capacity loss can leave an obsolete lease assigned temporarily; it cannot authorize processing beyond the new capacity.

This is a safety result under explicit assumptions, not liveness. For eventual completion one would additionally need persistent communication, sufficient budget, recurrent feasible capacity, successful motion/IK and a fair complete scheduling mechanism. Those hypotheses are not established by the greedy experiment. A fixed task set, obstacle navigation and joint saturation can prevent convergence. The positive reference mass ledger and its complementary blind-space result are independent of those physical outcomes.

## Fair comparison and measured counterexamples

Same-state replay isolates assignment: every method uses the identical existing trajectory, map quadrature and planar optical test. Private visibility bit masks are actually packed and routed. Its pose ceiling ignores capacity and is only an upper bound, not a solved capacitated optimum.

Closed-loop missions pair initial generalized states, targets, seeds, weights, MuJoCo model, actuator limits, detector, carrier and budget. Cyclic and order-k Voronoi baselines retain centroid references; repaired methods use prospective viewing poses and queued task execution. Thus this second comparison measures an allocation/control stack. The additional execution-aware geometric baseline prevents attributing generic feasibility planning gains exclusively to cyclic algebra. H is the SAME full-map nearest-k mean squared-distance cost for every fleet, including all demand. Input curves report actual velocity and native arm torque; absolute actuator work is not electrical energy. Slot count and weighted assigned-load imbalance are different quantities. Load imbalance is n/a after all active work finishes, rather than a spurious zero-error certificate.

The reference experiment (seed2718,45 s,N6,k2) completes weighted demand51.02% for cyclic,84.79% for Voronoi and100% for both repaired stacks. Final H is8.973,8.590,13.246 and10.265 m squared respectively. Byte-hops are903832,971376,1382600 and958136. The geometric repair baseline has lower H and traffic than cyclic repair here. Across three paired initial-position seeds both repaired stacks finish this task set; these three seeds are evidence, not a population-level statistical claim.

Other recorded cases vary N5/k2 (d1), N6/k3 (d3), announced N7->6/k2 (d1->2), a18000 byte-hop budget, a disconnected range carrier and an infeasible k1 two-view task. At N6,k3 the cyclic repair completes69.87% and geometric repair92.43% within45 s. A tight budget can make the more expensive repair protocol perform worse than the simple cyclic baseline. Disconnection and k1 leave zero confirmed completion. These counterexamples separate arithmetic blindness from resource/geometry/network infeasibility.

## Research direction

The clean paper contribution is a cyclic reference with exact quotient/Fourier classification, together with an explicit capability-constrained task hypergraph and a measured symmetry-breaking repair cost. Communication must pay for the private information it uses. Proving a distributed approximation or liveness result requires stronger scheduling conditions than this demonstrator. The [MIT ACL CBBA project](https://acl.mit.edu/projects/consensus-based-bundle-algorithm) is relevant background for bidding/consensus, but CBBA approximation guarantees are not transferred to this coupled coalition selection. The [higher-order coverage formulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.02424) motivates the full nearest-k geometric baseline, which optimizes a different objective from constrained multi-view execution.
