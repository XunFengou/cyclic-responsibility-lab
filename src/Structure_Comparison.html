Where does cyclic structure help?

Nearest-k Voronoi minimizes distance cost at fixed robot positions. Cyclic responsibility offers explicit arithmetic modes, a fixed family of owner sets, and simple moment-passing control. These experiments test what those properties buy, how much geometric cost they incur, and when their communication advantage disappears.

A structural benefit to test

Predict the blind space from gcd(N,k), repair it with complementary orders, and carry sufficient moments over a stable logical graph. Test equal-load exposure to a failed robot and the protocol's actual packet cost.

A strong geometric countercheck

Capacity-constrained geometric assignment can also balance workload. We compute a certified fractional optimum at fixed positions, rather than treating plain Voronoi's unequal loads as an inherent limitation of every Voronoi-type method.

A communication condition

If a full position and boundary snapshot is already supplied for safety, both responsibility maps can be computed locally from the known demand map. The current physical viewer then has the same full-snapshot cost for both methods.

A common special case: on a regular robot ring with uniform angular demand and correctly aligned rays, cyclic k-windows and order-k Voronoi coincide. The recorded symmetry countercheck verifies their moments and costs agree. Distance superiority is therefore not always strict.

2. Compare protocols on exactly the same position sequence

Replay a short recorded, safe single-integrator trajectory. Every protocol sees the same timestamps, demand quadrature, physical positions, cyclic boundaries, carrier and speed bound. This isolates communication from differences in closed-loop robot motion.

Per-epoch communication rounds

One packet per directed carrier edge per round; no wireless-interference or propagation-delay model. Startup discovery can dominate short runs.

What each protocol is allowed to know

Cyclic nodes know their own mass-coordinate boundary, the common demand map, and messages received over the carrier. Neighbor boundary exchange and window-moment tokens produce their exact responsibility moments; routed load messages produce the certified circulant boundary update.

Cached Voronoi nodes know their own exact position and a timestamped peer cache. A known speed cap gives an uncertainty ball for each peer. A dominant-region membership is accepted only when distance bounds certify it. Otherwise exact peer positions are requested and returned through routed packets. Every computed moment is checked against a fresh nearest-k reference. A high certified fraction does not imply few messages: uncertainty near many cell boundaries can touch many peers.

This is a responsibility-layer comparison. It does not implement a local-only obstacle / robot safety controller. If the full safety snapshot is already shared, each method needs zero additional responsibility packets on a known map; do not add these replay costs on top of that snapshot and claim a full-stack saving.

3. Does the failed robot's location matter?

Keep all surviving positions fixed and remove each physical ID in turn. Cyclic atoms start with equal mass. The two methods therefore face the same geometric failure, without robot motion or post-fault optimization confounding the immediate effect.

Immediate replaced responsibility mass

Turnover = integral of newly assigned memberships / k. At fixed surviving positions, both single-removal rules make one replacement only where the failed robot was responsible. Equal cyclic loads give turnover 1/N for every ID.

Equal dimension does not imply equal amplitude

Separate mass-only test: N=50 to 48, k=6. All patterns have d=6 and five blind dimensions. Their projections can differ because deletion positions change the mass perturbation.

4. Could a geometric method also balance the load?

Yes. At the same fixed positions, solve a centralized capacity-constrained minimum-cost flow on the common quadrature. Each point supplies k times its mass, each point-to-robot edge can carry at most that point's mass, and every robot receives k/N. Fractional splitting at quadrature cells is allowed. A residual-network certificate checks optimality.
