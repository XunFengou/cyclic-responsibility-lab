Precisely what is modeled
Vendor-derived planar-supported TIAGo Pro inertias and articulated links, ideal bounded actuators, experimental actuated parallel jaws and an ideal post-contact closure. The jaws replace fixed collision tooling; this is not a factory gripper or friction-only slip validation. Known obstacle map and exact simulated poses are used. Robot/self occlusion, total power-loss recovery, grasp search on unknown shapes and ROS hardware actuation remain outside the model.
The experiment demonstrates conditional protocol safety and measured numerical feasibility. It does not prove universal pickup success, optimal scheduling or collision-free whole-body convergence. Failed physics or missing evidence remains visible as an incomplete workflow.
