What this experiment can establish
The closed loop establishes causal precedence and tests actual shared dynamics, resource exclusion and routed evidence. Cyclic arithmetic classifies the reference operator; effective task eligibility and the dependency graph decide whether a workflow can finish. It does not prove universal scheduling liveness, optimality, collision-free whole-body convergence or hardware operation.
Known map, exact simulated observations, ideal pre-grasps, planar chassis support and conservative reserved workcell are explicit assumptions. Autonomous pickup, re-grasp transfer, finger slip, robot/self occlusion and ROS actuation are outside this experiment.
