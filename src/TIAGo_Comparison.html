Why a fleet of TIAGo Pro robots?

One independently controlled mobile manipulator is one agent. Its 20 coordinates are internal state, not 20 agents. K-th responsibility assigns each inspection location to k distinct physical robots, allowing simultaneous independent views and reassignment after a robot leaves. Torso/head positioning and arm inspection motions are then tasks inside each robot's controller.

The actual application is cooperative inspection allocation. A logical assignment does not guarantee a feasible view or equal service time: geometry, payload, joint range, saturation and obstacles matter. This experiment exposes that gap. Equal atom masses are not an optimal heterogeneous capacity allocation.

Scope: the PAL-published inertia and articulated tree are retained. The model adds planar support and ideal bounded chassis actuators; it does not simulate Mecanum rollers, slip, suspension, chassis tipping, calibrated cameras or identified factory control loops. Mesh visuals are removed, primitive collision geometry remains. Soft contact may penetrate slightly. Neither collision-free convergence nor hardware deployment is claimed.
