Topology, feasibility, and the two clocks

The responsibility hypergraph, boundary-control graph, and communication carrier are separate. Auxiliary orders modify boundary control while the service order stays fixed. The plant equations use motion time. The clock that adds communication rounds is cost accounting; it is not a delayed-plant or forced-stop simulation for robots with inertia.

Scientific assumptions

Atom moments come from a shared known static demand map, and overlapping windows are assembled through explicit routed packets. Epochs use complete reliable snapshots. Initial derivatives of integrator models are zero; role transfers preserve robot state. Unicycle and differential-drive models use a numerical goal deadband of 10^-8 domain units. The Euler-Lagrange example is a fully actuated planar two-coordinate mechanism with exact known inertia, Coriolis terms, potential, and damping.

Virtual rays are independent of robot polar angles. Nonempty sectors follow from the positive boundary update. Pairwise collision avoidance, autonomous failure detection, parked failed robots, unknown private sensing maps, and asynchronous delayed physical-plant stability are not implemented. Cartesian higher-order and nonholonomic models do not inherit the annular chart's containment proof.
